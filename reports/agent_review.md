## Method and limitations

- Research score is the mean of available growth, quality, valuation and technical scores; missing data is excluded rather than treated as zero.
- This is a triage score, not a forecast, fair value, or buy recommendation.
- Third-party summary data and headlines can be missing, delayed, or incorrect. Verify filings and IR releases from primary sources.
- The system does not place orders, size positions, or automatically change scoring weights.
